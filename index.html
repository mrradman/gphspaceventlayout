        How to use

        • Add items: drag from the left panel onto the plan, or click an item then click the plan (Esc to stop placing).
        • Select: click; Shift-click to add; Shift-drag on empty space to box-select; Ctrl+A selects all. Clicking a linked chair selects its whole group so it moves together; Alt+click picks a single chair.
        • Screens: set each fixed screen Up or Down in the right panel. A screen that's down shows as a blue bar.
        • Move: drag, or arrow keys (Shift = 500 mm).
        • Rotate items: R / Shift+R (15°), or the buttons in the Selection panel.
        • Duplicate: Ctrl+D. Delete: Delete / Backspace.
        • Link chairs: select 4–16 chairs and press L (or “Link”) — NSW I4D20 requires loose chairs on a level floor to be secured in groups of 4–16.
        • Pan: drag empty space. Zoom: mouse wheel / +−. Fit: F.
        • Rotate the view: ⟲ ⟳ in the toolbar. Exports use the current view rotation.
        • Measure: M, then click two points.
        • Undo / redo: Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y.
